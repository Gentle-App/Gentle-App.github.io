Download

Get Gentle on your Android phone

Gentle isn't on Google Play yet. Until it is, you can download it here and install it yourself. It's the same free app, and it takes a couple of minutes.

Download Gentle for Android

Version 1.0.2 · 52 MB · Android 8.0 or later · What's new

Having trouble installing it? Email me at gentleappdev@gmail.com and I'll help you get it working.

Open this page on the phone you want Gentle on. On a computer, you can send yourself the link instead: gentle-app.github.io/download.html

Have a Wear OS watch? There's an optional watch app too. Already using Gentle 1.0.1 or earlier? Move your data across first.

Already have Gentle from this website?

Gentle is getting ready for Google Play. The name Android uses to tell apps apart, com.gentle.app, was already taken there, so from version 1.0.2 Gentle is com.gentlepacing.app. Android sees that as a different app, so the new version installs next to your old Gentle instead of over the top. Moving your data across takes a couple of minutes.

  1. In your current Gentle, go to Tools, then Report for my doctor, and tap Back up everything. Save the file somewhere easy to find, such as Downloads.
  2. Download and install the new version, using the steps below. You'll have two Gentles on your phone for a little while.
  3. Open the new one. When it asks you to set up, tap Explore with sample data first.
  4. Go to Tools, then Report for my doctor, tap Restore a backup and pick the file you saved. Every entry, note, watch reading and setting comes back.
  5. Go to Settings, tap What Gentle may read and allow your health data again. If you had weekly automatic backups, turn them on again and choose the folder.
  6. When you've checked everything is there, uninstall the old Gentle. To tell them apart, press and hold an icon and tap App info, where the old one says version 1.0.1 or earlier.

If you use the watch app, install the new one too, using the watch steps below, and uninstall the old one from your watch. The new watch app only talks to the new phone app.

After this, new versions install over the top again, and your data stays where it is.

How to install it

Android is careful with apps that don't come from Google Play, so it asks a few extra questions along the way. This is what to tap.

  1. Tap Download Gentle for Android above. If your browser warns that this type of file can harm your device, tap Download anyway. Android shows that warning for every app downloaded from outside Google Play.
  2. When the download has finished, tap Open. If you missed it, open your Files app, go to Downloads and tap Gentle.apk.
  3. Your phone may say it isn't allowed to install unknown apps from this source. Tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then go back. You only need to do this once, and you can switch it off again when Gentle is installed.
  4. Tap Install.
  5. Google Play Protect may step in here, because it hasn't seen Gentle before. If it suggests scanning the app first, you can let it scan, or choose to install without scanning. If it says it has blocked the app, or that it doesn't recognise the developer, tap More details (a small arrow under the message), then Install anyway. Google keeps that button tucked away, so it's easy to miss.
  6. Tap Open. Gentle asks which health data it can read through Health Connect. Choose what you're happy to share, and you can change it later.

On Android 13 or older, you'll also need the Health Connect app from Google Play. Newer versions of Android have it built in.

Stuck?

If a step doesn't match what your phone shows, or Gentle won't install, email me at gentleappdev@gmail.com. Tell me your phone model and which step you're on, and send a screenshot if you can. I'll help you get it working.

The watch app (optional)

Gentle works without it, so skip this if you like. But if you have a Wear OS watch, such as a Pixel Watch or a Galaxy Watch 4 or newer, the watch app makes Gentle quicker and more accurate through the day. It reads your heart rate and steps straight from the watch every few minutes, so your points and alerts are up to date instead of waiting an hour or two for your watch's own app to sync. Your wrist buzzes when you go over your pace line, and your points left are on your wrist too.

Installing it is fiddlier than the phone app, because watches don't have an "allow unknown apps" switch. You'll need a computer for about ten minutes, or a helper app on your phone. Install Gentle on your phone first.

Installing it from a computer

  1. Connect the watch to the same Wi-Fi as your computer.
  2. Turn on developer options on the watch. Open Settings, then System, then About (and Versions, if you see it), and tap Build number again and again until it says you're a developer. On a Galaxy Watch, it's Settings, then About watch, then Software information, and you tap Software version.
  3. In Settings, open Developer options and switch on ADB debugging and Wireless debugging. Then tap Pair new device. The watch shows a pairing code and an address, something like 192.168.1.20:41234.
  4. On your computer, download Google's free SDK Platform Tools, unzip them, and open a terminal or command prompt in that folder. Type adb pair followed by the address from the watch, and enter the pairing code when it asks.
  5. On the watch, go back to the Wireless debugging screen and look for the address under IP address and port. It has a different port from the pairing one. On the computer, type adb connect followed by that address.
  6. Type adb install followed by the path to the Gentle-Watch.apk file you downloaded. When it says Success, open Gentle on the watch and allow it to read your heart rate and activity, including in the background. It finds Gentle on your phone by itself.
  7. Switch Wireless debugging and developer options off again. You won't need them any more.

Or from your phone

If you'd rather not use a computer, apps on Google Play such as Wear Installer 2 can send an APK to your watch over the same Wireless debugging connection. Do steps 1 to 3 on the watch, then follow the app's instructions to pair and send Gentle-Watch.apk. These apps are made by other people, not by me.

Getting updates

Apps installed this way don't update themselves. When there's a new version it'll be on this page, so download it and install it over the top in the same way, on your phone and, if you use it, on your watch. Your data stays where it is. The one exception is moving from 1.0.1 or earlier to 1.0.2, which has its own steps at the top of this page.

Once Gentle is on Google Play, you'll find it there instead, and this page will go.

If something doesn't work once Gentle is installed, please tell me. In the app, tap More, then "Report a problem", or email gentleappdev@gmail.com.

Questions about downloading

Is it safe to install an app from outside Google Play?

Only download Gentle from this page. The file is signed with my developer key, and Android only installs updates signed with the same key, so nobody else can slip in a changed version later. If you'd like to check the file, its SHA-256 is 902982e967d71c6314f6c94f9b692d3d80cdeb277a7c1ee644a4e6cbb7ecabaa and the signing certificate's SHA-256 is d7cf4823d718b2c4c07671e9f706d13e106669703e88440f60e0c0a468c38f07.

Why does my phone warn me about it?

Android warns about every app that doesn't come from Google Play, whoever made it. The warning doesn't mean anything has been found wrong with Gentle.

Can I put it on my watch?

Yes, if it's a Wear OS watch, such as a Pixel Watch or a Galaxy Watch 4 or newer. The watch app is optional and takes a few extra steps to install, which are above. Without it, Gentle still gets your watch's data through Health Connect.

Does it cost anything?

No. Gentle is free, with no subscription, and the version on this page has everything in it.

I got stuck. Can you help?

Yes, please email gentleappdev@gmail.com with your phone model and the step you got stuck on. A screenshot helps too.
