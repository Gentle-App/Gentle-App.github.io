Visible's cost

How much a Visible membership costs, and a free alternative

Visible is a pacing app for ME/CFS, Long COVID and other energy-limiting illnesses. The app is free to download, but its energy points and heart-rate alerts need a membership, which costs $19.99 a month, and Visible's own band. Gentle is a free pacing app for Android that does those things with no membership and the watch you already have.

How much does a Visible membership cost?

A monthly Visible membership is $19.99 a month. Over a year that comes to $239.88. Visible also sells yearly and two-year memberships, and every membership renews by itself until you cancel it.

The band is extra. Visible's Starter Kit, which includes the band, is a one-off cost on top of the membership, and the membership's main features don't work without it.

Visible doesn't list its prices on its website, so you only see them once you start signing up. The membership is only sold in the US and the UK.

Can I get the Visible app for free?

You can download Visible and use it without paying, but the free version is mostly a symptom tracker. It has daily check-ins and a morning heart-rate reading with your phone's camera, and you can join Visible's research studies from it.

PacePoints, Visible's daily energy budget, needs a membership. So do heart-rate tracking through the day, activity insights and pacing alerts. Without an active membership, Visible's band won't connect, and you can't see any of its data.

Does Visible work with my own watch?

Visible's membership features use its own band. Visible's help pages say the band doesn't connect to an Apple Watch, Garmin or Fitbit, and Visible doesn't use other smartwatches for PacePoints or alerts. If you already own a watch, you'd still need to buy Visible's band.

Gentle uses the watch or ring you already have, through Health Connect. That includes Pixel Watch, Fitbit, Samsung Galaxy Watch, Garmin, Withings, Oura, Polar and Amazfit. It can also connect to Bluetooth heart-rate straps and armbands, such as the Polar Verity Sense. Watches and Health Connect has the full list.

A free alternative to Visible

Gentle has these for free.

  • A daily energy budget in points, set each morning from your sleep, heart rate, HRV and how you say you feel. How the points work explains it.
  • Heart-rate alerts when you've been over your pace line for most of a stretch of time, on your phone and, with the optional watch app, on your wrist.
  • A morning stability score from 1 to 5.
  • A crash and PEM log that learns what usually comes before your crashes and warns you when it sees the same signs.
  • A PDF report and spreadsheets to take to your doctor.

You don't pay for a membership or a band. Gentle shows small banner ads, and a one-off 99p removes them. That's the only thing you can pay for, and it's staying that way.

When Visible might suit you better

Gentle is only on Android for now, so if you have an iPhone, it isn't an option yet. Visible works on both. Visible also gives you a live link to share with your clinician and research studies you can join. Gentle keeps your data on your phone, so it has neither of those.

Moving from Visible to Gentle

If you're on Android, you can keep using the watch you have. When you set Gentle up, it reads the last month of your watch data from Health Connect, so it doesn't start from nothing. You can download Gentle here until it's on Google Play.

Questions about Visible's cost

How much does a Visible membership cost?

A monthly membership is $19.99 a month, which is $239.88 over a year. Visible's band is a one-off cost on top of that. Visible also has yearly and two-year memberships, and they all renew automatically until you cancel.

Can I get the Visible app for free?

Yes, but only the basic version. The free app has daily check-ins and a morning reading with your phone's camera. PacePoints, heart-rate tracking through the day and pacing alerts need a paid membership and Visible's band. Gentle has a daily energy budget and heart-rate alerts for free.

Can I use a Visible membership without the band?

Not for its main features. PacePoints, heart-rate tracking and alerts need the band, and the band needs a membership to work. Without an active membership, you can't connect the band or see its data.

Does Visible work with Apple Watch, Garmin or Fitbit?

Visible's help pages say its band doesn't connect to Apple Watch, Garmin or Fitbit, and its membership features use the band. Gentle reads Garmin, Fitbit, Pixel Watch, Samsung and other watches through Health Connect, but it's Android only, so it doesn't work with an Apple Watch.

Is there a Visible app for Android?

Yes, Visible is on Android and iPhone. If you're on Android and don't want to pay for a membership, Gentle is a free pacing app with energy points, heart-rate alerts and a crash tracker.

Is there a free alternative to Visible?

Gentle is free and works with the watch you already have, and there's no premium version to pay for. It's on Android, and you can download it here until it's on Google Play.
