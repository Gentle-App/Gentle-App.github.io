Endometriosis, adenomyosis and PCOS

Pacing your energy with endometriosis or PCOS

Endometriosis can leave you exhausted as well as in pain, and with PCOS your periods can turn up whenever they like. Gentle is a free app that tracks your energy, your pain and your cycle together, so you can see which days tend to be harder and plan lighter ones around them.

Why endometriosis wears you out

Fatigue is common with endometriosis. In a study published in Human Reproduction in 2018, about half of the women with endometriosis said they often felt fatigued, compared with about a fifth of the women without it. Pain, heavy periods and broken sleep all add to it.

Gentle doesn't diagnose or treat anything. It shows how your energy, pain and cycle line up over the weeks, which gives you and your GP or gynaecologist a real record to look at.

Pain through your cycle

Log your periods, from spotting to heavy, and log pain whenever you have it, with where it is, what kind of pain and how bad. There's an area for the pelvis or lower tummy. Gentle then checks whether your period, the five days before it or the middle of your cycle are usually harder for you, going by how often you crash, how tired you are and how much pain you're in.

If one of them is, your energy allowance is 10% lower on those days, and Gentle tells you a few days ahead so you can plan lighter days. It needs a full cycle or two first. How the points work explains the allowance, and how you spend it through the day.

The symptoms NICE lists

When you choose endometriosis in the app, Gentle adds period pain, heavy bleeding or flooding, pain during sex, painful bowel movements and painful peeing to your evening check-in. These are the symptoms NICE's endometriosis guideline (NG73) lists as reasons to suspect it. Adenomyosis adds the first three. Any symptoms you already track stay as they are, and you rate each one as none, mild, moderate or severe.

Your answers build up into a picture of how often each symptom comes and how bad it gets, and they go into the report for your doctor.

A report for your gynaecologist

The report is a PDF with charts, plus spreadsheets of everything you've logged. It includes your recent cycle lengths and a chart of your average pain on each day of your cycle, counted from the first day of a period, next to your fatigue, symptoms and any crashes. That's hard to remember accurately between appointments, so having it written down can help.

Irregular periods and PCOS

The Period screen lists your last eight cycles and how long each one was, with the shortest and longest. FIGO's guidance counts cycles as regular when the shortest and longest are no more than 7 to 9 days apart. If yours vary by more than 9 days, Gentle stops predicting your next period and doesn't try to say which days might be harder, because there's no way to know ahead of time. It still learns from each period once it's happened.

Cycles that vary this much, or long gaps between periods, are worth raising with your doctor, and they're in your report. If you wear a blood sugar sensor that shares with Health Connect, Gentle can read it as well and shows each day's average and lowest reading. Gentle doesn't track your weight.

Questions about endometriosis and PCOS

Is there a free app for endometriosis?

Gentle is free, with no subscription and no premium version. The pain log, period tracking, the check on which part of your cycle is harder, and the report for your doctor are all in the free app. It's on Android, and you can download it here until it's on Google Play.

Can Gentle track my pain through my cycle?

Yes. Log your pain and your periods, and Gentle checks whether your period, the days before it or the middle of your cycle are usually more painful for you. Your doctor's report shows your average pain on each day of your cycle.

Does it work if my periods are irregular?

Yes. Gentle shows how long each cycle was. If they vary by more than 9 days, it stops predicting your next period, because a guess would often be wrong, and it keeps learning from the periods you log.

Does Gentle track weight?

No. Gentle only reads the health data it uses, and nothing in it uses your weight.

Can it tell me if I have endometriosis or PCOS?

No. Gentle is a self-management tool, not a medical device. If you think you might have either, see your GP, and take your Gentle report with you if it helps.

Is any of this shared with anyone?

Not unless you share it. Everything stays on your phone, and it only leaves if you send it somewhere yourself, such as the report for your doctor.
