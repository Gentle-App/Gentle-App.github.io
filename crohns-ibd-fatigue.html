Crohn's and IBD

Pacing for Crohn's and IBD fatigue

With Crohn's or colitis, the tiredness can be as hard to live with as the gut symptoms, and each makes the other worse. Gentle tracks both, so you can see how your bad gut days and your low-energy days line up.

Energy works the same as for any fatigue condition

At the heart of Gentle is a daily allowance of energy points. It's set each morning from your sleep, your heart rate and how you say you feel, and you spend it through the day on moving about, heart-rate strain and mental effort. That part is the same whatever your condition, and how the points work goes through it in full.

If you choose "Crohn's / IBD" when you set Gentle up, it suggests extra gut symptoms to track in your check-ins. It also adds suggestions written for IBD, such as getting the fixable causes of tiredness checked (anaemia is one), with links to the NICE and ECCO guidance they come from.

Gut symptoms can lower today's allowance

If you tick a symptom in "How are you right now?" and it's worse than usual for you, today's allowance comes down a little: 5% for a moderate symptom and 10% for a severe one, up to 15% at most. Only the symptoms likely to cost energy count, such as abdominal pain, diarrhoea, bowel urgency, nausea, bloating, joint pain and brain fog.

It has to be worse than usual for you. If you have mild bloating most days, it won't keep taking points off. Gentle compares against the middle of your last 30 evening check-ins, and mild symptoms never count.

Bowel movements and food

Log each time you go, with the type on the Bristol stool chart and anything worth noting, like urgency, blood, mucus, pain or straining. A chart shows how often you've been going over two weeks, a month or three months, coloured by type.

You can keep a list of foods you can't eat, and Gentle reminds you when you log one. If your food log and your gut log show a link, Gentle points it out. In the sample data, gluten was followed by loose or urgent bowel movements 43% of the time, compared with 17% without it. You can add anything it finds to your list with one tap.

All of this goes into the report for your IBD team: bowel movements by type, foods that cause problems, and symptoms and fatigue over time.

Other things you can track alongside it

You can log medications and supplements with the date you started each one, so you can compare how things were before and after. Some people find their gut symptoms and fatigue change with their period, so Gentle can track your cycle and check whether part of it is harder for you. You can also log aches and pains, including joint pain, with where they are and how bad, and keep typed or spoken notes with the date and time.

Questions about Crohn's and IBD

Can Gentle tell me which foods cause my flares?

It can show links in your own logs, such as a food that's often followed by loose bowel movements, but that doesn't prove the food caused them. Cutting out whole food groups without advice can leave you short of nutrients, so check with your IBD team or a dietitian first.

Does it work for colitis, IBS or coeliac disease?

Yes. Anyone can use the bowel tracker, food diary and list of foods you can't eat. The condition you can pick in the app is "Crohn's / IBD", and if yours isn't listed, pick "Other / not sure" and use the same screens.

What if I have a flare?

Log it as a symptom flare, or as a crash if you need to stop and rest. A crash takes 20% off today's allowance, and either way it shows up in your history and your report. Gut symptoms that are worse than usual can lower the allowance too.

Is any of this shared with anyone?

Not unless you share it. Everything stays on your phone, and it only leaves if you send it somewhere yourself, such as the report for your doctor.
