Crohn's and colitis

Crohn's and colitis fatigue, and your gut, in one app

Fatigue is one of the hardest parts of Crohn's disease and ulcerative colitis, and it doesn't always lift when a flare settles. Gentle is a free app that tracks your energy and your gut symptoms together, so you can see how your bad gut days and your low-energy days line up.

Why Crohn's and colitis can make you so tired

Tiredness with IBD has several possible causes, from inflammation and poor sleep to anaemia and low iron or vitamin levels. Some of these can be treated, so it's worth asking your IBD team to check. If you choose "Crohn's / IBD" when you set Gentle up, it suggests the same, with links to the NICE and ECCO guidance the advice comes from.

Gentle can't tell you which cause applies to you. What it can do is show how your energy, sleep, symptoms and food change from day to day, which gives you and your IBD team something concrete to look at.

Pacing your energy with IBD

At the heart of Gentle is a daily allowance of energy points. It's set each morning from your sleep, your heart rate and how you say you feel, and you spend it through the day on moving about, heart-rate strain and mental effort. Pacing yourself against that number is how you avoid overdoing it on a good day and paying for it on the next one. How the points work goes through it in full.

An IBD symptom tracker that knows your usual

If you choose "Crohn's / IBD" when you set Gentle up, it suggests extra gut symptoms to track in your check-ins. When you tick a symptom in "How are you right now?" and it's worse than usual for you, today's allowance comes down a little: 5% for a moderate symptom and 10% for a severe one, up to 15% at most. Only the symptoms likely to cost energy count, such as abdominal pain, diarrhoea, bowel urgency, nausea, bloating, joint pain and brain fog.

It has to be worse than usual for you. If you have mild bloating most days, it won't keep taking points off. Gentle compares against the middle of your last 30 evening check-ins, and mild symptoms never count.

Bowel movements, food and trigger foods

Log each time you go, with the type on the Bristol stool chart and anything worth noting, like urgency, blood, mucus, pain or straining. A chart shows how often you've been going over two weeks, a month or three months, coloured by type.

The food diary is quick: type or say what you had, and Gentle tags it. Keep a list of the foods that trigger your symptoms, and Gentle reminds you when you log one. If your food log and your gut log show a link, Gentle points it out. In the sample data, gluten was followed by loose or urgent bowel movements 43% of the time, compared with 17% without it. You can add anything it finds to your list with one tap.

Tracking flares

Log a flare when it starts, or afterwards, with how bad it is and anything you think set it off. It shows in your history alongside your sleep, energy, food and bowel movements from the days before, and when it eases you can mark it as over. If you need to stop and rest, log it as a crash instead, and today's allowance drops by 20% to protect your recovery.

Everything goes into a report for your IBD team: a PDF with charts of bowel movements by type, symptoms and fatigue over time, any flares, and the foods that cause problems, plus spreadsheets of it all.

Other things you can track alongside it

You can log medications and supplements with the date you started each one, so you can compare how things were before and after. Some people find their gut symptoms and fatigue change with their period, so Gentle can track your cycle and check whether part of it is harder for you. You can also log aches and pains, including joint pain, with where they are and how bad, and keep typed or spoken notes with the date and time.

Questions about Crohn's and colitis

Is there a free app for tracking Crohn's and colitis?

Gentle is free, with no subscription and no premium version. The symptom tracker, bowel movement chart, food diary, trigger-food list and report for your IBD team are all in the free app. It's on Android, and you can download it here until it's on Google Play.

Why am I so tired with Crohn's, even when it's under control?

Fatigue is common with Crohn's and colitis, including when the gut is quiet, and it can have causes worth checking, such as anaemia or low iron. Ask your IBD team. Gentle helps by showing how your energy, sleep and symptoms change over time, which you can take to your appointment.

Can Gentle tell me which foods trigger my Crohn's?

It can show links in your own logs, such as a food that's often followed by loose bowel movements, but that doesn't prove the food caused them. Cutting out whole food groups without advice can leave you short of nutrients, so check with your IBD team or a dietitian first.

How do I track a Crohn's or colitis flare?

Log it as a symptom flare, or as a crash if you need to stop and rest. A crash takes 20% off today's allowance, and either way it shows up in your history and your report. Gut symptoms that are worse than usual can lower the allowance too.

Does it work for ulcerative colitis, IBS or coeliac disease?

Yes. Anyone can use the bowel tracker, food diary and list of trigger foods. The condition you can pick in the app is "Crohn's / IBD", which covers ulcerative colitis, and if yours isn't listed, pick "Other / not sure" and use the same screens.

Is any of this shared with anyone?

Not unless you share it. Everything stays on your phone, and it only leaves if you send it somewhere yourself, such as the report for your doctor.
