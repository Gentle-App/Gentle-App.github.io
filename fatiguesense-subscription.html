FatigueSense's cost

FatigueSense's subscription, and a free alternative

FatigueSense is a pacing app that started out free. It now needs a subscription, FatigueSense Plus, at £5.99 a month to keep using it. Gentle is a free pacing app for Android with a daily energy budget, heart-rate alerts and a crash tracker, and it will never have a subscription.

Is FatigueSense still free?

It isn't any more. FatigueSense began as a free app, developed as part of PhD research at Newcastle University. In November 2026 it brought in FatigueSense Plus, a subscription of £5.99 a month, and you need it to carry on using the app.

The app stores still describe FatigueSense as free to download, because the subscription is paid inside the app. Its App Store listing shows two in-app purchases: FatigueSense Plus at £5.99 and FatigueSense Plus Supporter at £9.99.

How much does FatigueSense cost?

FatigueSense Plus is £5.99 a month, which comes to £71.88 a year. You also need a FatigueSense account.

A lot of people who need a pacing app can't work much, or at all. I don't think paying every month to keep track of your own energy should be one more cost of being ill, which is why Gentle is free and staying free.

What FatigueSense does

FatigueSense gives you a daily energy budget, warns you when you're near your limit, and looks for early signs of PEM. It's on iPhone and Android and works with a wide range of wearables, including Apple Watch, Garmin, Fitbit, Oura and WHOOP. You can also use it with no wearable, by entering things by hand.

A free alternative to FatigueSense

Gentle does the same job for free.

  • A daily energy budget in points, set each morning from your sleep, heart rate, HRV and how you say you feel. How the points work explains it.
  • Heart-rate alerts when you've been over your pace line for most of a stretch of time, on your phone and, with the optional watch app, on your wrist.
  • A crash and PEM log that learns what usually comes before your crashes, and when your payback tends to arrive.
  • Planning your day, and a PDF report and spreadsheets for your doctor.

It also has things I couldn't find in FatigueSense: medications, period tracking, a bowel movement and food tracker, check-ins by voice that work offline, and themes with a low-stimulus mode.

There's no subscription and no account, and your data stays on your phone. Gentle shows small banner ads, and a one-off 99p removes them. That's the only thing you can pay for, and it's staying that way.

When FatigueSense might suit you better

Gentle is only on Android for now, so if you have an iPhone or an Apple Watch, it isn't an option yet. FatigueSense works on both, and it connects to some wearables that don't send data to Health Connect.

Moving from FatigueSense to Gentle

If you're on Android, you can keep using the watch you have. When you set Gentle up, it reads the last month of your watch data from Health Connect, so it doesn't start from nothing. You can download Gentle here until it's on Google Play.

Questions about FatigueSense's cost

Is FatigueSense free?

It isn't any more. It started out free, but since November 2026 it needs FatigueSense Plus, a subscription of £5.99 a month. It's still free to download, because the subscription is paid inside the app.

How much does FatigueSense Plus cost?

£5.99 a month, which is £71.88 a year. The App Store also lists a FatigueSense Plus Supporter option at £9.99.

Can I keep using FatigueSense without paying?

No. FatigueSense Plus is needed to carry on using the app. If you're on Android, Gentle is a free alternative with an energy budget, heart-rate alerts and a crash tracker.

Is there a free alternative to FatigueSense?

Gentle is free, with no subscription and no premium version. It's on Android, and you can download it here until it's on Google Play.

Does Gentle work with my watch?

Most likely, if it sends data to Health Connect. That includes Pixel Watch, Fitbit, Samsung Galaxy Watch, Garmin, Withings, Oura, Polar and Amazfit. Watches and Health Connect has the details.

Will Gentle bring in a subscription too?

No, and that's a promise. Gentle won't ever have a subscription or premium features.
