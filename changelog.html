Changelog

What's new in Gentle

I've listed every change to Gentle here, newest first, back to the first version, which went on the phone of someone close to me on 27 October 2026. If you want to know why something changed, email me at gentleappdev@gmail.com and I'll explain.

Times are UK time. To update, download the new version from the download page and install it over the top, and your data stays where it is. The one exception is moving to 1.0.2, which installs as a separate app, so you bring your data across with a backup. Since 31 October, whenever I've changed how the numbers are worked out, there's been a switch in Settings to go back to the old way.

Version 1.0.2

Phone app 1.0.2 and watch app 1.0.1, 23 November 2026, 7:45pm.

Gentle is getting ready for Google Play. The name Android uses to tell apps apart, com.gentle.app, was already taken there, so from this version Gentle is com.gentlepacing.app. Android sees that as a different app, so 1.0.2 installs next to your old Gentle instead of over the top. The download page has the steps for moving your data across, and it only takes a couple of minutes.

  • Restoring a backup made by an older version of Gentle brings back every entry, note, watch reading and setting.
  • If you've already set Gentle up, it now asks "Replace what's in Gentle?" before a backup replaces anything.
  • If you had weekly automatic backups turned on, Gentle tells you to choose the folder again after restoring, because the new app needs your permission to save there.
  • After a restore, Gentle reads your watch history from Health Connect again, once you've allowed it to.
  • Gentle no longer asks Health Connect for calories, distance, floors climbed, body temperature or weight. It never used them.
  • Live heart rate connects straight away to your strap or armband, including ones whose Bluetooth address keeps changing, which some sensors do for privacy. Before, Gentle couldn't connect to those at all.
  • If the connection to your sensor drops, Gentle keeps trying to reconnect until it's back.
  • Settings has a new Ads section, ready for when Gentle is on Google Play. Until then there are no ads.
  • Once Gentle is on Google Play, small banner ads appear at the bottom of the five main screens, just above the tabs. They never pop up or cover anything, and the ad code never gets your health data.
  • Remove ads in Settings takes them away for good for a one-off 99p. Have a code? lets you redeem a code from me in the Play Store, and Restore purchase finds a Remove ads you've already bought, for example on a new phone.
  • Ads only appear when Google Play can offer Remove ads on your phone, so there's always a way to take them away.
  • In the UK and EU, Google's consent form asks about personalised ads before the first one, and Ad privacy choices in Settings lets you change your answer.
  • Settings, under Privacy, has a link to the full privacy policy. The Privacy screen has a new section about ads and a button that opens the full policy.
  • Help has a new article, "Why are there ads, and how do I remove them?".
  • The watch app is now 1.0.1. It has the same new name, so it works with the new phone app and not the old one. Install it and uninstall the old watch app. Nothing else in it has changed.
  • Points, allowances and alerts work exactly as they did in 1.0.1.

Version 1.0.1

Phone app, 22 November 2026, 5:36pm. The watch app is still 1.0.0.

  • The first time you open Gentle each day, it now sends me an anonymous count, so I can see how many people use it, how often, for how long and which parts. The count holds the date, your version of Gentle and how many of the last 30 days you opened it. For the last day you used it, it also says how many minutes Gentle was open and which screens you opened, by name, such as Today or Food. There's no ID in it, and nothing you've logged or anything from your watch.
  • You can turn the count off in Settings, under Privacy, with "Send an anonymous daily count". Once it's off, nothing is sent and Gentle stops keeping track for it.
  • What the count keeps on your phone is stored separately, so it never goes into a backup, an export or a problem report.
  • Gentle checks once a day whether there's a newer version, and Today shows a note with a link to the download page when there is. The check sends nothing about you, and you can turn it off in Settings, under Privacy, with "Tell me when there's a new version". Settings also has a "Check for a new version" button, and shows which version you have.
  • The Privacy screen explains the count and the version check, and Help has a new article, "What's the anonymous daily count?".
  • Points, allowances and alerts work exactly as they did in 1.0.0.

21 November 2026, 2:21am

  • On the chart, a small ring marks a day you said you hadn't been. A day with nothing logged is left empty, because it's unknown, and a red dot marks a day with blood noted. Tap a day to open it, and the line underneath gives the average, the most in a day and how many days had loose ones.

  • When you log a food that's on your list, Gentle tells you and still lets you add it, and Today marks it "On your can't-eat list". Similar foods count as well, so a cheese toastie gets flagged if milk is on your list, but broad labels like high fibre don't.
  • If your food and bowel logs show a link, say dairy often followed by loose bowel movements, Gentle offers to add that food to your list.
  • Your list goes in the report for your doctor and the spreadsheets, and the AI helper knows about it.
  • "Send report" and the zip option on the problem report screen fill in gentleappdev@gmail.com and the subject line in your email app. Nothing is sent until you press send.
  • Help has two new articles, "Can I see how often I've been going?" and "Can I keep a list of foods I can't eat?".

8 November 2026, 1:15pm

  • Today's points now count from when you're up. Gentle takes the first sign of it, such as last night's sleep ending, a check-in, a note, a food or bowel log, or the first hour with 300 or more steps, and assumes nothing before that. It used to count an ordinary amount from 7am, so you could see points used before you'd got out of bed. Early shifts work too. You can switch back in Settings, under Pacing, with "Count my day from when I'm up".
  • A crash or PEM that started on an earlier day and was over by your morning check-in now belongs to the day it started, and today counts as the day after one. Before, both days were crash days and today's allowance was cut twice. The switch is "A crash that ends overnight counts for the day it began", under Pacing.
  • The Gentle watch app's steps count from 5am, when Gentle's day starts, so steps between midnight and 5am aren't counted for two days. The switch is "Count watch steps from 5am", under Watch data.
  • Pace-line alerts and the 80% and 100% budget alerts follow your day, from when you're up until your usual bedtime, which Gentle works out from your last 14 nights. They used to stop at 10pm.
  • The "higher PEM risk today" notification comes in your first 4 hours up, where it used to come at 8 to 11am.
  • The heart-rate notification looks back over the last 15, 30, 45, 60, 90 and 120 minutes, and tells you when you've been over your pace line for two-thirds of one of them, such as "12 of the last 15 minutes" or "40 of the last 60". It only goes out with a fresh reading, and at most once every half hour. Before this it hardly ever went off.
  • Problem reports show these settings and when you got up.
  • Help has two new articles, "Why had I used points before I'd even got up?" and "How does the 'heart rate over your pace line' notification work?".

7 November 2026, 12:01pm

  • Gentle learns what your steps really cost you. It looks at your big step days, one and a half times your ordinary day or more, and checks the two days after each for a crash, PEM or a check-in clearly more tired than usual, then compares that with your other days.
  • If big days don't catch up with you any more than other days, steps beyond your ordinary day cost less, down to half the usual rate, up to the most steps you've done without payback. Steps past that cost full price, and steps never cost more than they did before.
  • It needs 4 big days and 5 other days with check-ins after them, and only takes off the full discount once it has 8 big days.
  • Tools, then What Gentle has learned, shows what it found or what it still needs, and the "Moving about" line in your allowance shows the price.
  • The switch is "What steps really cost you", under What Gentle learns, and there's a new Help article, "My busy days (like work shifts) use lots of points".

  • Opening Gentle on the watch starts it again if it had stopped, and tells you when it last sent anything.
  • If your phone hasn't heard from the watch app for 30 minutes, it nudges it to start again.
  • Today tells you what's holding your data up. Either the watch app hasn't sent anything since a certain time, with how to start it, or Google Health hasn't passed anything on, with a link that opens it so it syncs.
  • Pulling down to refresh with nothing new says which of those it is, and problem reports show whether the watch app is working.
  • Help has a new article, "Why does it say there's no new watch data since earlier?".

  • Today only re-reads your watch data every 2 minutes while it's on screen.
  • Log has a History screen with every day back to the first, with the points used of that day's allowance, tiredness, steps, sleep and everything you logged. You can search it and filter it by kind, and the Points filter has a chart of the last 30 days with crash days marked.
  • Gentle keeps each day's allowance, and days from before this have an estimate marked "allowance worked out afterwards".
  • A day's view shows the allowance that day, check-in details, when crashes ended and the plan items you ticked off.

31 October 2026, 10:23pm

  • Symptoms you tick in "How are you right now?" can take a little off today's allowance when they're worse than usual for you. A moderate symptom takes off 5% and a severe one 10%, up to 15% in all, and mild ones never count. Your usual is worked out from your last 30 evening check-ins without a crash.
  • Only symptoms likely to cost energy count, such as gut flare symptoms, pain, dizziness, breathlessness and brain fog. Allergies only count when they're severe, and symptoms you've added yourself never change the allowance.
  • A symptom that often comes before your crashes counts half as much again.
  • Nothing counts twice. If your tiredness answers already lowered the allowance, only the bigger cut applies, and a crash day's cut covers symptoms too.

Feeling worse than an ordinary day still lowers it, and feeling better still raises it. The switch is "Feeling okay never lowers my allowance", under Pacing.
  • You can give your own number for an ordinary day's steps, under What Gentle learns, or say something like "I usually do about 4,000 steps".
