Privacy

Your data stays on your phone

Everything you log in Gentle, and everything it reads from your watch, is stored on your phone. It isn't sent to me or to anyone else. There's no Gentle account, and no Gentle server to send it to.

Last updated 21 November 2026.

The main points

  • Your health data is stored on your phone and never shared with anyone, me included.
  • The only time I see any of it is if you send me a bug report and choose to include your data. I use it to fix the problem, delete it once the bug is fixed, and never pass it to anyone else.
  • Gentle reads from Health Connect and never writes to it.
  • There's no analytics or tracking inside the app.
  • The ads never get your health data.

What Gentle keeps, and where

Gentle keeps your check-ins, notes, crashes, food and bowel logs, plans and settings, and the watch data it has read, in its own private storage on your phone, where other apps can't read it. Uninstalling Gentle deletes it all, and there's also a button in Settings that deletes everything without uninstalling.

If you make a backup or a report for your doctor, the file goes wherever you choose to save or send it.

What Gentle reads from Health Connect

With your permission, Gentle reads steps, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, exercise sessions, calories, distance, floors, blood oxygen, breathing rate, skin and body temperature, menstruation, weight, nutrition and hydration. You choose which of these it can read, and you can change that in Health Connect whenever you like. Gentle only uses them on your phone, to work out your energy points, alerts and patterns.

The watch app

The Gentle watch app sends heart rate and steps from your watch to Gentle on your paired phone, and the phone sends back your points left and your pace line. That data only goes between your own watch and phone.

When anything leaves your phone

Only in these cases, and only when you choose.

Bug reports

Please send one whenever something seems wrong, however small. In the app, tap More, then "Report a problem", and Gentle builds a report you can read before sending. It can include technical details, today's energy figures, your notes, recent voice notes and a copy of your Gentle data, and each of those has its own switch. They all start switched on, because the more I can see, the faster I can fix the problem, so switch off anything you'd rather not send. Your AI key is never included.

The report goes from your own email app to gentleappdev@gmail.com. I only use it to find and fix the problem, I delete it once the bug is fixed, and it's never passed to anyone else.

The AI helper, if you set one up

The AI helper is off unless you turn it on, and there are two kinds. One runs on your phone: Gentle downloads a language model once, and after that nothing about you is sent anywhere. The other runs online, with your own key from a provider you pick, such as Google Gemini or Groq. When you ask that one something, your question and a summary of your data go to the provider, under their terms. Gentle tells you this when you set it up.

Voice notes

Voice notes use your phone's own speech recognition service, which on most Android phones is Google's. Depending on your phone and its settings, your speech may be turned into text on the phone or by that service. Gentle only receives the words, and works out what you meant on your phone.

Ads

Gentle shows small banner ads through Google AdMob. To show an ad, AdMob receives standard information about your device, such as an advertising identifier, your IP address and your device type. It never receives your health data or anything you've logged. In the UK and EU, Gentle asks for your consent before any ads are personalised. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, and a one-off 99p removes the ads completely.

The 99p purchase

Google Play handles the payment, so Gentle never sees your card details. All it learns is whether you've bought "Remove ads".

This website

None of this section is about the app, which has no analytics. It's only about this website.

The website uses Google Analytics (through Google Tag Manager) and Microsoft Clarity, which show me which pages people read and where they get stuck. They set cookies and collect things like the pages you visit, your browser and device, roughly where you are, and, for Clarity, how you scroll and click. Google and Microsoft handle this data under their own privacy policies.

It also uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without storing anything on your device. It only sees things like which page was visited, the browser and device type, and roughly which country you're in.

You can turn them off whenever you like, from the notice on your first visit or with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page. Turning them off removes their cookies, and they won't load again in that browser. Your choice, and the theme you pick, are saved in your own browser.
