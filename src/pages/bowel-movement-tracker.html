Gut and food

A bowel movement tracker that doesn't make it a chore

Logging one takes two taps, and a chart shows how often you've been going. There's a food diary too, and a list of foods you can't eat that warns you when you log one. It's all in the same app as your energy, so you can see how they line up.

Logging a bowel movement

Pick the closest type on the Bristol stool chart. Each of the seven types has a small picture, from separate hard lumps (type 1) to watery with no solid pieces (type 7). Types 3 and 4 count as typical, 1 and 2 as hard, 5 as soft, and 6 and 7 as loose. You can leave it blank if you'd rather not say.

Then add anything else that applies: urgent, blood, mucus, painful, straining or not fully emptied. You can change the time if it was earlier, and add a note for your doctor.

If you tick blood, Gentle reminds you it's worth telling your doctor, especially if it's new or keeps happening.

On a day you haven't been, tap "I haven't been today". Gentle treats a day with nothing logged as unknown, so it only counts as a day without one if you say so.

You can also say it: "loose poo this morning, really urgent" logs type 6 with urgency.

How often, as a chart

The bowel screen has a bar for each day, over two weeks, 30 days or 90 days. Each bar is split by type, so loose days stand out in red and hard ones in a darker colour, and a dashed line shows your average.

A small ring marks a day you said you hadn't been, and a red dot marks a day you noted blood. Tap any bar to open that day.

Underneath, Gentle adds it up. For the sample person that's about 1.5 a day, with the most in one day and how many days had loose ones. If your loose days line up with higher fatigue, it tells you that too.

Foods you can't eat

Keep a list of the foods that cause you trouble. For each one you can note what it does to you (diarrhoea, urgency, stomach pain, bloating, wind, nausea, reflux or a flare) and how bad it is, from "a little" to "can't have it at all". Common ones like dairy, onion and garlic, spicy food and sweetcorn can be added with a tap.

When you log a food that's on your list, the food screen tells you, but still lets you add it, because sometimes you eat it anyway. It also catches foods of the same kind, so if milk is on your list and you log a cheese toastie, it points out that cheese counts as dairy.

You can add to the list by voice too. "Onions don't agree with me" or "spicy food gives me diarrhoea" adds the food and what it does, and "I can eat bread again" takes one off.

Food diary and gut links

The food diary is optional and quick. Type or say what you had, and Gentle tags it for you as dairy, gluten or wheat, caffeine, spicy, fried or fatty, high fibre and so on. You can change the tags if they're wrong.

After about a week of logging food alongside bowel movements or gut symptoms, Gentle starts looking for links. It compares days when you had a food with days you didn't, looking at loose or urgent bowel movements, stomach pain and reflux on that day and the next. A link only shows up when the difference is big enough, and then Gentle offers to add the food to your list.

A link in your logs doesn't prove the food caused it. Check with your doctor or a dietitian before cutting out whole food groups.

Questions about tracking bowel movements

What is the Bristol stool chart?

It's a seven-point scale doctors use to describe stool, from type 1 (separate hard lumps) to type 7 (watery, with no solid pieces). Types 3 and 4 are generally typical. Using it means "a bit loose" means the same thing to you and your doctor.

Can my doctor see my log?

Only if you choose to show them. The report for your doctor has a bowel section with a chart of how often you went, a count of each type and anything you noted, plus your list of foods that cause problems. You decide who gets it.

Why doesn't a day with nothing logged count as zero?

Most people don't log every day, and an unlogged day would look like constipation. Tap "I haven't been today" when it's true, and Gentle will count it.

Is this private?

Yes. It stays on your phone like everything else in Gentle. The privacy page has more.
