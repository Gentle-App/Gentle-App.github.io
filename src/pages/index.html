Free pacing app for Android

Today's energy, counted for you

Gentle takes what your watch already records, adds a few taps from you, and works out how much energy you have for the day. It counts down as you go, tells you when your heart rate has been high for a while, and over time it learns what tends to happen before you crash.

Free, with no subscription. Needs Android 8.0 or later. Works with Pixel Watch, Fitbit, Samsung, Garmin and others through Health Connect, or with no watch at all.

Who it's for

Gentle is for anyone who has to ration their energy. Usually that's because of a long-term illness, especially the energy-limiting kind, where doing too much on Tuesday means paying for it on Thursday. You don't need a diagnosis to use it. When you set it up you can pick ME/CFS, Long COVID, post-viral fatigue, multiple sclerosis, cancer-related fatigue, rheumatoid arthritis, lupus, fibromyalgia, POTS, Ehlers-Danlos, Crohn's and IBD or Parkinson's, or "Other / not sure".

If you know spoon theory, you already know how this works. You start the day with a limited amount and everything you do uses some of it. Gentle uses points instead of spoons and keeps count for you, so you don't use up energy keeping track.

A day with Gentle

  1. In the morning, you answer three questions with a tap each: how tired you feel, how much energy you have and how you slept. Gentle adds last night's sleep, heart rate and HRV from your watch, gives the morning a stability score from 1 to 5, and sets today's allowance from that.
  2. During the day you don't have to do anything. Steps, and time with your heart rate over your pace line, come off your points as they happen. Thinking, talking and noise use points too, at an ordinary rate until you rate them in the evening.
  3. If your heart rate has been over your line for most of the last 15 minutes, your phone tells you. With the Gentle watch app, your wrist buzzes as well.
  4. Whenever you like, you can tap "How are you right now?" or say it out loud: "Had lunch, then crashed at 3, still in it." Gentle splits that into a meal, a crash and a check-in, and checks with you before saving.
  5. In the evening, if you're up to it, you can note your symptoms, what you did and how hard it felt. That goes into tomorrow's numbers and into what Gentle learns about you.

What's in it

Energy points

100 points is your ordinary day, worked out from your last 30 days. Half of it is for moving about, a quarter for heart-rate strain and a quarter for thinking and seeing people.

How the points work

Heart-rate pacing

A pace line based on your resting heart rate. When you've been over it for a while, the alert says how long, for example "12 of the last 15 minutes".

Heart-rate pacing

Crashes and PEM

Log a crash when it happens or afterwards. Gentle shows what usually came before yours and warns you when those signs turn up again.

The crash log

Gut and food

Log bowel movements on the Bristol stool chart and see how often you go. There's a food diary too, and a list of foods you can't eat that warns you if you log one.

Gut and food tracking

Your watch, or none

Gentle reads from Health Connect, so most watches work, and Pixel and Galaxy watches can run an optional Gentle app. With no watch, your check-ins do the work.

Watches and Health Connect

For your doctor

A PDF with charts, and spreadsheets of everything you've logged. Useful for appointments where you won't have the energy to explain it all from memory.

History

Every day since you started, and you can search it. Type "headache" and you'll see every day you mentioned one.

Themes and a dim mode

Low-stimulus mode for bad days, larger text if you want it, and a choice of themes, Y2K sparkle and Frutiger Aero among them.

Made by one person

Gentle started as an app for someone close to me who lives with a long-term illness. A lot of what's in it was added because it was needed on a particular day: the bowel movement chart, the list of foods to avoid, and alerts that can keep going through a late evening, if needed, instead of stopping at 10pm.

It's still made by one person, and making money isn't the point, so this is how it'll stay:

  • Everything in Gentle is free.
  • There's no subscription or premium version, and there never will be.
  • The only ads are small banners. You won't get pop-ups or full-screen video ads.
  • You can pay 99p once to remove the banners. It's the only thing for sale.
  • Your data stays on your phone. Nobody else sees it, me included.

Getting Gentle

Gentle is coming to Google Play. It needs an Android phone with Android 8.0 or later, and the optional watch app needs Wear OS 3 or later. There's no iPhone version yet, and the questions below say why.

If you'd like to try it before then, or you have a question, email gentleappdev@gmail.com.

Bugs and ideas are always welcome

If anything looks wrong, I want to hear about it, even if it's a typo or a number that seems a bit off. There's only me, and I can't test Gentle on every phone, so your report is often the only way I'll find out. In the app, go to More, then "Report a problem". It fills in the technical details for you, and you can read it before it's sent. Or email gentleappdev@gmail.com.

Feature requests are very welcome too. If there's something you wish Gentle did, or something that would make a bad day a bit easier, email me and tell me about it. Plenty of what's in Gentle was added because someone needed it.

Questions people ask

Is Gentle really free?

Yes, every feature. There are small banner ads, which you can remove with a one-off payment of 99p (99 cents in the US). There's no subscription and no premium version, and there won't ever be.

Do I need a smartwatch?

You don't. A watch lets Gentle count your steps and heart rate for you. Without one, your morning check-in still sets the allowance, and you can plan your day and log what you did. The numbers are rougher, but you can still pace with it.

Which watches work with Gentle?

Any watch, ring or band whose app shares with Health Connect: Pixel Watch and Fitbit (through Google Health), Samsung Galaxy Watch, Garmin, Withings, Polar, Amazfit and the Oura ring. The optional Gentle watch app runs on Wear OS watches such as Pixel Watch and Galaxy Watch 4 or newer.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. I'd like to make one, but Apple charges $99 a year to publish apps on the App Store, so the Android version would need to bring in enough to pay for that first.

Where does my data go?

It stays on your phone, and it isn't sent to me or anyone else. It only leaves if you send it yourself. For example, if you send a bug report and tick the box to include your data, I'll use it to fix the problem and then delete it. The privacy page has the details.

I found a small bug. Is it worth reporting?

Yes, please. Small ones are worth reporting too. Use More, then "Report a problem" in the app, or email gentleappdev@gmail.com.

Can I suggest a new feature?

Yes, please do. Email gentleappdev@gmail.com with what you'd like Gentle to do and what it would help you with. I'd love to hear it.

Can Gentle tell me what's wrong with me?

It can't, because it isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose anything. What it can do is help you see your own patterns and stay within your limits, and it can make a report for your doctor.

What if I'm too unwell to fill things in?

That's completely fine. Nothing in Gentle is compulsory, so on a bad day you can leave it be. It keeps going on whatever your watch sends, low-stimulus mode keeps the screen dim, and if you do feel like checking in, you can say it out loud instead of tapping.
