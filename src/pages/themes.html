Themes

Calm on bad days, sparkly on good ones

Light sensitivity and brain fog both change what you can look at. So Gentle has a very dim mode for the worst days, plain light and dark themes for most days, and a few looks that are only there because they're fun.

This website has the same themes, and the same low-stimulus switch. You'll find both under the Theme button at the top of the page.

There's also Y2K bling, and "Match my phone", which follows your phone's light or dark setting.

Low-stimulus mode

One switch in Settings makes everything very dim, with muted colours and nothing that moves or sparkles. It works on top of whichever theme you've picked, so you can turn it on for a bad day and off again without losing your theme. It's meant for light sensitivity, migraines, sensory overload, or any day when bright things are too much.

Other settings that help

Text can be normal, large or larger. Reduce motion keeps every animation still, and on the decorated themes, Gentle twinkle lets the sparkles and bubbles drift slowly or not at all. You can also turn vibration on or off.

Questions about themes

Do the themes cost anything?

They don't. Every theme is free, like everything else in Gentle, and none of them are locked behind a payment.

Why are there three versions of Y2K sparkle and Frutiger Aero?

Those looks were redesigned over time, and the earlier versions are still there in case you prefer them. In Settings you pick the look, then the version underneath it. The newest one is picked to start with.

Does low-stimulus mode change my numbers?

Not at all. It only changes how Gentle looks.
