What it costs

Free, with no subscription, for good

Gentle is made by one person, to help people who struggle to manage their energy. Making money isn't the point of it, and how it pays its way fits on this page.

What you get, and what it costs

  • Every feature in Gentle is free: the points, the alerts, the watch app, the crash log, the gut tracking, the doctor's report and the themes.
  • The app shows small banner ads.
  • A one-off payment of 99p (99 cents in the US) removes the banners for good. It's the only thing you can buy.

What will never happen

  • A subscription of any kind, including a "Gentle Plus".
  • Premium features. Nothing in the app is locked behind a payment, and nothing will be.
  • Pop-up ads, full-screen ads or video ads.
  • Selling your data, or using your health data for advertising. Your health data doesn't leave your phone unless you send it somewhere yourself.

What's the catch?

There isn't one.

Gentle started as an app for someone close to me who lives with a long-term illness. Then FatigueSense, another pacing app, began bringing in a £5.99-a-month subscription that you need just to use the app. Visible, another one, keeps its energy points and heart-rate alerts for paying members, and those only work with Visible's own band or a Polar armband.

A lot of people who need to pace can't work much, or at all. I don't think paying every month to keep track of your own energy should be one more cost of being ill, and that's what pushed me to put Gentle out for everyone, for free.

The ads

The banner ads are how everything else stays free for everyone. They sit at the edge of the screen, and they don't pop up, play sound or cover anything.

If they bother you, 99p gets rid of them. If 99p is too much right now, keep the ads, and you won't miss out on anything.

About the 99p

It's a single payment through Google Play, linked to your Google account. If you get a new phone or reinstall Gentle on the same account, it comes back by itself, and you don't need a Gentle account for that. Google Play handles the payment, so Gentle never sees your card details.

Who makes Gentle

It's just me. Gentle started as an app for someone close to me with a long-term illness, and most of what's in it was added because it was needed on a particular day.

If something doesn't work, or you've got an idea, email gentleappdev@gmail.com and it comes straight to me. Feature requests are very welcome, big or small. Please report bugs even if they seem too small to bother with. There's only one of me and I can't test Gentle on every phone, so your report is often how I find out. In the app, More, then "Report a problem" does most of the work for you.

Questions about cost

Is Gentle free?

Yes, all of it. The only payment is an optional 99p to remove the banner ads.

Will you add a subscription later?

No, and that's a promise. Gentle won't ever have a subscription or premium features.

Do the ads see my health data?

They don't. Your health data stays on your phone and isn't passed to the ad service. Like any banner ad, it gets basic information about your device so it can show an ad, and the privacy page explains what that is.

Will the ads get in the way?

They're small banners. Nothing pops up or covers the screen, and nothing plays sound or video.

I bought "remove ads" and got a new phone. Do I pay again?

You don't. Sign in with the same Google account and it comes back by itself. There's also a "Restore purchase" button.
