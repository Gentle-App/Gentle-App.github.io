Watches

Works with the watch you already have

You don't need to buy anything to use Gentle. It reads from Health Connect, which is where watch and fitness apps on Android share their data, so if your watch's app shares there, Gentle can use it.

Which watches

Gentle knows these apps, and can open each one for you when its data is running late:

Other watches can work too, as long as their app shares with Health Connect. What Gentle gets depends on what each app shares. Almost all of them share steps and heart rate, but sleep, HRV and resting heart rate vary.

From Health Connect, Gentle can read steps, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, breathing rate, blood oxygen, skin temperature, exercise sessions, nutrition and hydration. You choose which of these it's allowed to read, and it never writes anything back.

When your data is running late

Watch apps pass your data to Health Connect in batches, after they've synced with the watch. Sometimes a batch takes an hour or two, and then everything arrives at once.

When that happens, Gentle tells you, with something like "Nothing new from Google Health since 3:27pm". Tap it and Gentle opens Google Health, which usually makes it sync, and reads again a minute later. You can also pull down on the Today screen to check.

Steps are counted the way Google Health counts them: your watch's steps while you're wearing it, and your phone's when you're not, so the same walk isn't counted twice.

The Gentle watch app

If you have a Wear OS watch, such as a Pixel Watch or a Galaxy Watch 4 or newer, there's an optional Gentle app for it. It reads heart rate and steps straight from the watch every few minutes, so it doesn't have to wait for Google Health to sync.

It shows your points left for today, and your heart rate against your pace line, on your wrist. It buzzes when a reading is over your pace line, at most every 5 minutes. If the watch restarts, the app starts sending again by itself, and the phone gives it a nudge if it goes quiet.

Sleep, HRV and resting heart rate still come through Health Connect, because watch apps aren't allowed to read those directly.

The watch app installs separately from the phone app. Once Gentle is on your phone, you'll find it in the Play Store on your watch.

A chest strap, for live heart rate

Gentle can also connect to a Bluetooth heart-rate chest strap or armband. That gives a reading every second, and an alert within a minute of going over your line. While it's connected, Gentle keeps a small notification showing.

Or no watch at all

Gentle works without a watch. The morning check-in sets your allowance, and you can plan your day and log what you did. Steps can come from your phone's own step counter through Health Connect.

Questions about watches

Does Gentle work with an Apple Watch?

Not at the moment. Gentle is an Android app that reads from Android's Health Connect, and an Apple Watch only pairs with an iPhone. I'd like to make an iPhone version, but Apple charges $99 a year to publish on the App Store, so the Android version would need to bring in enough to cover that first.

Why don't my steps match my watch exactly?

Usually it's because your watch's app hasn't passed on its latest batch yet. Also, Gentle's energy day runs to 5am unless you change it, so steps after midnight count toward the day you were still living, while the steps shown on screen run from midnight to midnight like your watch.

My sleep or HRV is missing. What do I do?

Check that your watch's app is sharing those types with Health Connect, and that Gentle is allowed to read them. In Google Health, new types can stay switched off until you approve them. In Gentle, Settings, then "Connect your watch" checks everything and tells you what's arriving.

Does the watch app drain the battery?

It uses the watch's own passive health monitoring, which is designed to run all day, and sends small batches every few minutes instead of keeping a connection open.
